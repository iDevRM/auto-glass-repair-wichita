What Does ADAS Calibration Involve?

ADAS stands for advanced driver assistance systems. On most newer cars, a camera mounted behind the windshield near the mirror watches lane lines and the cars ahead.

After a windshield replacement, calibration in Wichita means setting the car on level ground, placing a target at an exact distance, and using a scan tool to tell the camera where straight ahead is. Some cars also need a short road drive to finish.

When Do You Need Calibration?

  • After any windshield replacement on a car with a forward camera
  • When a lane-keep or collision warning shows up on the dash after glass work
  • If lane-keep assist pulls the car toward the line instead of away
  • After an alignment or suspension work, on some makes

A common question: "My old windshield never needed this, why now?" Older cars had no camera on the glass. If yours was built in the last several years, it probably does.

Why Does a New Windshield Throw the Camera Off?

The camera bracket is bonded to the glass. Every new windshield sits a tiny bit differently in the frame, and the camera moves with it.

A one-degree aim error doesn't sound like much, but at highway speed on Kellogg that can put the camera's idea of the lane a few feet off by the time it looks 100 yards ahead. Calibration fixes that.

Static vs. Dynamic Calibration: Which Does Your Car Need?

Static calibration happens parked, using printed targets set at measured distances in front of the car. Dynamic calibration happens on the road, with the scan tool connected while the car drives at set speeds on clearly marked lanes.

Many cars need one, and some need both. We look it up by VIN before your Wichita windshield replacement so we arrive with the right setup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every car need ADAS calibration after windshield replacement?

No, only cars with a camera or sensor mounted to the windshield. Most cars built in the last several years have one for lane-keep assist or automatic braking, and we check by VIN before every Wichita windshield job.

What happens if I skip ADAS calibration?

The camera may aim slightly off, so lane-keep assist, collision warnings and automatic braking can react late or at the wrong time. Some cars shut the features off and show a warning light until the camera is calibrated.

How long does ADAS calibration take in Wichita?

Most calibrations take 30 to 60 minutes after the windshield is installed. Dynamic calibrations need a short drive on well-marked Wichita roads, which adds a little time.
